## Brand voice

- **We are**: direct, execution-focused, operator-owned, safety-first, no-nonsense. The copy consistently leads with *capability and control* ("in-house crews," "direct control over labor and production," "self-performed") rather than soft/aspirational language.
- **We are not**: flowery, vague, or subcontractor-dependent-sounding. Avoid language that implies outsourcing or brokering work — "self-performed" and "in-house" are load-bearing words, use them deliberately.
- **Tone**: confident and plainspoken. Headlines are short, punchy, all-caps sentence fragments ("EXECUTION-DRIVEN ROOFING," "OPERATOR-OWNED"), not full marketing sentences.

## Messaging pillars (confirmed, from the Capabilities Statement)

1. **Self-performed / in-house execution** — dedicated crews, not subcontracted labor
